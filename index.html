Paperback Kindle

Every kid needs a friend who just listens. His happens to be a tiny pink axolotl.

Meet Avocado. A curious boy finds him in a mountain stream, brings him home, and tells him everything. About school. About monsters under the bed. About the things he's afraid to tell anyone else.

Avocado never interrupted. He never laughed. He never said, "That's silly." He just listened.

And when listening turns into adventure, a cardboard box becomes a spaceship, a blanket becomes a mountain, and a little aquarium becomes an entire world.

Written by a dad, for the son who named Avocado.

About Connor

I'm a dad, a designer and an artist in Utah. Avocado the Axolotl started the way it does in the book: I told my son, "It's an axolotl," and he said, "An Avocado?" That was that.

I write the kind of stories I want to read to my own kids at bedtime: gentle, a little funny, and honest about big feelings. More are on the way.
